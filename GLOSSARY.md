# Patient Registration

Terms used to describe automated patient registration and its outcomes.

## Language

**Patient ID**:
The identifier assigned to a patient in Moonlight.

**Registration success**:
An outcome where a Patient ID was confirmed absent before a registration attempt and present after the save attempt.

**Unresolved registration**:
A registration for which a save was attempted but the outcome has not been confirmed.

**Duplicate Patient ID**:
A Patient ID already present before an attempt to register it.
